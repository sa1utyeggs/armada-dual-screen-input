## Reddit — r/AynThor

### English

#### Title

I made a bottom-screen keyboard and touchpad for my Thor on ArmadaOS

#### Body

Hey everyone! I wanted to type and control the top-screen pointer from my Thor’s bottom screen, so I’ve been working on an open-source app called **Armada Dual Screen Input**.

It runs in Steam Game Mode, with typing, tap-to-click, two-finger scrolling and touch modes. English and Chinese Pinyin input are supported.

I’ve tested it on my own Thor with ArmadaOS. Longer sessions and suspend/resume still need testing, so I’d love to hear how it works for others.

🎬 [46-second demo](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sa1utyeggs/armada-dual-screen-input/main/.github/assets/interface-demo.mp4) — typing, pointer control and Slay the Spire 2 footage, with bilingual captions and a gesture animation at the end.

🔗 [GitHub / downloads / installation](https://github.com/sa1utyeggs/armada-dual-screen-input)

Anyone else using ArmadaOS on their Thor? Curious what you’d use this for!
